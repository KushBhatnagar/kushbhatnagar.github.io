## The concept in plain words

A **context window** is everything an AI can "see" right now: the current chat, from the first message to the last. It's like scrolling up in a WhatsApp group to check what you missed.

But it only lasts while the chat is open. Start a new chat and it's a fresh WhatsApp chat, with no scroll history at all. And when the window fills up or resets, the AI can't see what dropped out of it.

**Persistent memory** is the notebook. Someone deliberately saves your name, preferences and past decisions outside the chat, then feeds them back in the next time. The AI doesn't keep anything by itself; it reads what was written down.

So when "the AI forgot what I told it", it usually isn't about intelligence. That conversation simply wasn't in front of it. Context window is what the AI sees right now; memory is what got written down for later.
