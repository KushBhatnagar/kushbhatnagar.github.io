ChatGPT doesn't plan its answers. It plays Antakshari.

Remember Antakshari at family functions?
Nobody plans the whole session. You hear the last letter and pick the best next song.

That's exactly how an LLM writes.
It looks at everything so far, asks "what usually comes next?", and picks one token.
Then it does it again. And again. Until the email is done.

No goal. No grand plan. Just superhuman next-move prediction, until it looks like thought.

For PMs, this one idea explains why outputs vary, why models hallucinate, and why context is the product.
