## The concept in plain words

A **reasoning model** is an AI model that writes out rough working before it gives you the final answer.

Think of board exams. Two students know the same syllabus, but the one who works it out on the rough sheet first usually scores better. Reasoning models do the same: instead of blurting the first answer, they break the question into steps, try an approach, check themselves, and only then answer.

This also clears up a common myth. A model doesn't get smarter from your chats: it's frozen after training and improves only when a new version is released. A lot of the recent jump came from this rough-sheet habit, not a bigger brain.

Underneath, it's still next-word prediction. The model simply predicts its own rough work first, and that becomes its plan. Same brain, more time to think.
